## Workflows

### Workflow 1 — Lead Capture Automation
**Status:** Complete
**Tools:** Google Forms, Google Sheets, Gmail
**Modules:** 3
![Workflow 1](Workflow%201%20%E2%80%94%20Lead%20Capture%20Automation.png)

**What it does:**
Automatically captures customer enquiries submitted via
Google Forms, saves the lead data to a Google Sheet, and
sends an instant email notification with the lead details.

**Real World Use Case:**
Any small business collecting customer enquiries online.
Instead of manually checking form responses, the business
owner gets an instant email alert the moment a new lead
comes in.

**What I learned:**
- How triggers work in Make.com
- How to map nested JSON values from Google Forms correctly
- The difference between Run Once and scheduled runs
- How to debug duplicate instance ID errors

### Workflow 2 — Invoice Alert System
**Status:** Complete
**Tools:** Google Sheets, Gmail
**Modules:** 4 + Filter
![Workflow 2](Workflow%202%20%E2%80%94%20Invoice%20Alert%20System.png)

**What it does:**
Monitors an Invoice Tracker Google Sheet for new unpaid
invoices. When a new unpaid row is added, it automatically
sends a professional payment reminder email to the client
and updates the invoice status to Reminder Sent so the
client is never emailed twice.

**Real World Use Case:**
Freelancers and small business owners who send invoices
regularly. Instead of manually chasing payments, the system
handles the first reminder automatically the moment an
invoice is logged.

**What I learned:**
- How to use Filters in Make.com
- How to update a specific row in Google Sheets dynamically
- How to map row numbers for precise sheet updates

### Workflow 3 — Multi-Touch Invoice Follow Up System
**Status:** Complete
**Tools:** Google Sheets, Gmail
**Modules:** 9 (3 Gmail + 3 Sleep + 2 Google Sheets Update + 1 Filter)
![Workflow 3](Workflow%203%20%E2%80%94%20Multi-Touch%20Invoice%20Follow%20Up%20System.png)

**What it does:**
An advanced invoice follow up system that sends a sequence
of 4 automated emails at timed intervals:

- Email 1: Instant payment reminder when invoice is added
- Email 2: Firm follow up after 3 days
- Email 3: Urgent reminder on the due date
- Email 4: Overdue warning one day after due date

The system automatically updates the invoice status after
the first and final emails so records stay accurate.

**Real World Use Case:**
Freelancers, consultants and agencies managing multiple
clients. Instead of manually tracking who has paid and
who has not, the system runs the entire follow up sequence
automatically from the moment an invoice is logged.

**What I learned:**
- How to use the Sleep module in Make.com
- How to convert days into seconds for delay calculations
- How to build multi-step sequential automation flows
- The limitations of Sleep modules and when to use
  scheduled scenarios instead
- How to structure status updates across a long running flow
